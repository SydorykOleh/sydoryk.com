# Interview Preparation: Technical Artist @ Yggdrasil

> [!NOTE]
> **Goal of a "Meet and Greet"**
> This initial meeting is typically about **culture fit, communication style, and mutual interest**. They want to know if you are proactive, communicative, and passionate about games. Keep your answers concise, friendly, and engaging.

## 🎯 Role Overview
- **Position:** Technical Artist
- **Core Mission:** Act as the bridge between artists and developers.
- **Key Themes:** Optimization, pixel perfection, directory structure, pipeline maintenance, asset integration (Unity), and collaboration (internal & external).

---

## 💡 Your Key Selling Points (How You Align)

When they ask about your background, highlight these specific overlaps between your experience and their requirements:

1. **The "Bridge" Role (Artist + Developer):** 
   - You have 9+ years of full CG production experience *and* a deep background in IT/DevSecOps. You speak both "Art" and "Code" fluently.
2. **Pipelines & Directory Structures:** 
   - They specifically mentioned "directory structure" and "optimal asset pipelines". Emphasize your experience engineering custom Python pipelines for cross-network file validation and directory organization.
3. **Engine Experience (Unreal -> Unity):**
   - Address the Unity requirement confidently. You led a transition to real-time pipelines in Unreal Engine 5. Emphasize that core concepts (asset integration, optimization, shaders, pipeline architecture) transfer perfectly to Unity.
4. **Tool Proficiency:** 
   - Mention your hands-on experience with **Spine** (working with animators), **Photoshop**, **Git**, and **Houdini VEX**. 
5. **Programming Skills (Bonus points):**
   - The job lists "Programming skills" as a *Nice to Have*. Make sure to mention your Python and Shell Scripting skills, as this puts you ahead of artists who lack coding experience.

---

## ❓ Questions They Might Ask You

**1. "Tell us about yourself and your background."**
* **Strategy:** Give a 2-minute elevator pitch. Mention your 9 years in CG production, your transition to real-time engines, and how your IT/DevOps background makes you uniquely suited for pipeline and technical art roles.

**2. "You have a lot of Unreal experience, but this role uses Unity. How comfortable are you with that?"**
* **Strategy:** Be transparent but confident. State that while your recent deep-dive was UE5, the fundamental principles of real-time rendering, material creation, draw-call optimization, and asset pipelines are engine-agnostic. 

**3. "How do you handle asset optimization and pipeline efficiency?"**
* **Strategy:** Reference your past work architecting render farms and writing custom Python scripts for file validation. Talk about how you empower artists by automating tedious tasks so they can focus on creativity.

**4. "Tell us about your experience working with external art studios or cross-functional teams."**
* **Strategy:** Highlight your experience at a lead/supervisor level where you likely had to validate assets, manage workflows, or provide technical guidance to other artists.

**5. "How do you ensure 'pixel perfection' and proper directory structure?"**
* **Strategy:** Explain your systematic approach. Mention your natural inclination to keep project folders organized and your technical scripts that enforce naming conventions or validate files across a network.

---

## 🗣️ Questions You Should Ask Them

Asking good questions shows you are proactive and genuinely interested in solving their problems.

> [!TIP]
> **Pick 2-3 questions from this list to ask at the end of the meeting.**

1. **Pipeline Challenges:** *"What is the biggest bottleneck in your current asset pipeline between artists and developers right now?"*
2. **Porting Projects:** *"I saw the role involves recreating graphics for porting projects. Are there specific platforms you are targeting that usually cause technical hurdles?"*
3. **Team Structure:** *"What does the dynamic look like between the internal team and the external art studios you partner with? How much of the technical validation falls on this role?"*
4. **Shaders (Nice to Have):** *"The job description mentioned Unity shaders as a 'nice to have.' Are you currently looking to build custom shaders for a specific upcoming game or feature?"*
5. **Success Metrics:** *"For someone coming into this role, what does success look like in the first 3-6 months?"*

---

## 🧠 Final "Meet & Greet" Tips

* **Be a Problem Solver:** The JD states they want someone "not afraid of technical challenges." Show enthusiasm for troubleshooting and finding new solutions.
* **Emphasize Empathy:** A TA is a bridge. Show that you understand the pain points of both developers (badly optimized assets) and artists (tools that are hard to use).
* **Vibe Check:** Smile, keep the energy positive, and let your passion for games and real-time tech shine through.
