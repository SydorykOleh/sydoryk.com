Hi,

I have over 9 years of experience in full CG movie production, real-time visualization, and procedural workflows, primarily working at a lead/supervisor level. 

I have a very sharp eye for lighting and mood, having lit and composited over 300 production shots. I designed studio-wide Arnold and Redshift workflows tailored for Maya, and authored master templates that standardized our compositing across multiple large-scale cinematic attractions.


I am also highly technical and experienced with Python pipeline development. I've engineered custom Python tools for everyday production use and integrated robust rendering pipelines. For the last few years, my workflow has been fully inside Houdini and Karma, making USD the core foundation of my daily work. Because of this procedural mindset and strong foundation in Python, adapting to your specific USD-based pipeline will be a very natural transition for me. I'm very comfortable collaborating closely with Look Development teams to ensure shaders and assets meet technical and artistic requirements.

I'm currently based in Krakow, Poland, but I am open to relocating to Tenerife for this role. I actually really enjoy the island - one of my recent projects, NaturaVision, was produced specifically for Loro Parque in Tenerife! I would love to continue this conversation so we can figure out which of my skills you need the most and how I can best contribute to the upcoming series "Messi and the Giants".

Cheers,  
Oleh
