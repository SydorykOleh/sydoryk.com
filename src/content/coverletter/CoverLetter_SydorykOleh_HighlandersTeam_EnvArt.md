Hi,

I have over 9 years of experience in full CG movie production, real-time visualization, and procedural workflows, primarily working at a lead/supervisor level. 

At my previous position, I led the transition of our traditional rendering pipeline to Unreal Engine 5. We produced multiple cinematic attractions entirely in Unreal. I was heavily involved in building procedural natural environments, generating terrains in Houdini for Unreal Landscapes, and utilizing PCG for asset scattering. I also focused heavily on engine performance optimization and working efficiently with ready-made assets. Here is an example of an Unreal project where I handled both the FX and cinematic lighting: https://sydoryk.com/detail/SunnyBunny

While I might not have direct experience working on hunting games, I have a passion for creating immersive, natural environments and refining scene atmosphere. I learn quickly and hope you can see my strong technical foundation and procedural mindset as a strong bridge to this role. 

I'm currently based in Krakow, Poland. I would love to continue this conversation so we can figure out which of my skills you need the most and how I can best contribute to your team.

Cheers,
