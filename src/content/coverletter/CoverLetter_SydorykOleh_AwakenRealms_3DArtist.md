Hi,

I have over 9 years of experience in CGI production, real-time visualization, and procedural workflows, primarily working at a lead and supervisor level.

I take assets from initial blockout all the way to final engine integration. I have a solid technical grasp of the full 3D pipeline, including high-to-low poly modeling, clean retopology, UV unwrapping, baking, and PBR texturing in Substance Painter. At my previous studio, I led the transition of our pipeline to Unreal Engine 5, where I set up master materials, managed lighting and atmosphere, and built real-time environments. Here is an example of an Unreal Engine project where I handled scene composition, lighting, and FX: https://sydoryk.com/detail/SunnyBunny

I also have extensive experience mentoring junior and mid-level artists, running daily reviews, and establishing technical guidelines so the team delivers consistent, engine-ready assets without bottlenecks. When it comes to fantasy aesthetics and atmospheric lookdev, here is an old unfinished cinematic project I am proud of that showcases my approach to mood and visual storytelling: https://youtu.be/3gb4lI7_XRA

While I don't have direct experience with sculpting, my primary DCC background is in Maya, Houdini, and Substance Painter, and I adapt quickly to new toolsets.

I am currently based in Krakow, Poland. I am available and would love to discuss how my artistic and technical background can support your upcoming projects at Awaken Realms.

Cheers,  
Oleh
