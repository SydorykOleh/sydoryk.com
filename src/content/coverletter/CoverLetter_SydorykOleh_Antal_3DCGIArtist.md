Hi,

I have over 9 years of experience in full CG movie production, real-time visualization, and procedural workflows, primarily working at a lead/supervisor level.

Recently, I've been heavily focused on high-fidelity product visualization and animation—ranging from interactive product configurators to industrial equipment and furniture. I'm experienced in transforming raw CAD data into production-ready geometry, establishing physically accurate PBR textures, and setting up custom studio lighting rigs. 

I rely heavily on Houdini and Solaris to build scalable setups and handle complex physical simulations. I've engineered fully automated pipelines for massive product visualization batch rendering, and I'm very comfortable setting up realistic VFX simulations like fluids, smoke, and particles. I also integrate AI tools for rapid ideation and post-production workflows where needed.

While my main DCC has been Maya and Houdini rather than 3ds Max with Corona/V-Ray, I learn quickly and hope you can see my strong foundation in lighting, look development, and procedural simulations as a strong bridge to this role. I can easily switch to 3ds Max and adapt to your pipeline.

I'm currently based in Krakow, Poland. I would love to continue this conversation so we can figure out which of my skills you need the most and how I can best contribute to your team.
