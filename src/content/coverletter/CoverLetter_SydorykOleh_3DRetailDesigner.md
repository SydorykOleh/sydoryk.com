You can view my full resume here: [Resume (PDF)](https://sydoryk.com/assets/resume_SydorykOleh_LRC.pdf)

I have over 9 years of experience in full CG movie production, real-time visualization, and procedural workflows, primarily working at a lead/supervisor level. 

Recently, I've been heavily focused on high-fidelity product visualization and configurators - ranging from interactive microphone and headphone configurators to industrial equipment and furniture. For a recent sofas and chairs project, I built a fully procedural pipeline that delivered over 8,000 images across multiple collections, variations, and clients. I'm experienced in transforming raw CAD data into production-ready geometry and establishing physically accurate PBR textures. I have a very sharp eye for lighting and mood, having lit and composited over 300 production shots, which I believe is crucial for creating engaging visual storytelling and customer shopping paths.

While I might not have direct experience with SketchUp or commercial retail scenography, I learn quickly and hope you can see my deep lighting expertise and high aesthetic sense as a strong bridge to this role. I have advanced skills in 3D, can create any 3D model from scratch, and have extensive experience working directly with CAD data. I can easily switch to SketchUp and AutoCAD as needed.

I'm currently based in Krakow, Poland. I am currently available and would love to discuss the position in more detail to see how I can best contribute to your team.

Oleh
