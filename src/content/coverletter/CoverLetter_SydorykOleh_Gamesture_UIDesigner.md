Hi,

I have a strong background in interactive design, 3D pipelines, and building automated tools that speed up creative production. I don't believe in manually pushing every single pixel when modern AI pipelines and structured design systems can produce dozens of high-quality UI variants in an hour.

Over the past few years, I have actively integrated AI and rapid prototyping into my daily workflow. I build custom scripts, vibe-code interactive web apps, and connect generative tools into pipelines that turn moodboards and references into functional UI screens, popups, and offer panels. On the technical side, I build scalable component systems and work closely with developers to make sure assets and layouts translate cleanly into engines like Unity.

Here is a practical example of an end-to-end automated procedural workflow I built. It takes a raw `.midi` file and automatically generates the final `.mp4` video with every single step automated:
- Physics simulation (rigid-body dynamics)
- Particle simulation
- Caching and geometry preparation
- Automated batch rendering
- Multi-pass compositing
- Audio synthesis timed directly to MIDI notes
- FFmpeg video encoding and optimization
- Automated upload and scheduling to social platforms using my custom web app, [MultiCast](https://github.com/SydorykOleh/MultiCast)
