Hi,

I have over 9 years of experience in full CG movie production, real-time visualization, and procedural workflows, primarily working at a lead/supervisor level.

At my previous position, I led the transition of our traditional rendering pipeline to Unreal Engine 5. We produced multiple attractions and movies entirely in Unreal. I was responsible for the lighting workflow, building procedural environments using PCG, and creating complex stylized FX with Niagara. Here is an example of an Unreal project where I handled both the FX and cinematic lighting: https://sydoryk.com/detail/SunnyBunny

I rely heavily on Houdini and Solaris to automate and scale up asset production. I've engineered fully automated pipelines using Houdini TOPs for massive product visualization batch rendering, and created procedural tools to generate complex FX or scatter vegetation. Here is a small example of tools created inside Houdini to be used directly in Unreal: https://sydoryk.com/detail/HoudiniTools

While I might not have direct experience with traditional game development and I don't have a lot of experience with organic sculpting in tools like Blender or Z-Brush, I learn quickly and hope you can see my strong foundation in procedural asset creation, look development, and Unreal Engine as a strong bridge to this role. I can easily switch to new tools as needed and I am very familiar with Perforce and optimizing assets for real-time performance. I also wanted to add that I usually don't use generative AI for creative work, but I do leverage it for workflow improvements and pipeline automation.

I'm currently based in Krakow, Poland. I would love to continue this conversation so we can figure out which of my skills you need the most and how I can best contribute to your team.

Cheers,  
Oleh
