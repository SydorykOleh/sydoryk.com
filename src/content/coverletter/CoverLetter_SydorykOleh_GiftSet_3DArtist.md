Hi,

For your corporate gift set, I can handle the complete 3D workflow from accurate dimension modeling to final photorealistic rendering:
- Accurate 3D modeling for the 180 g candle jar with wooden lid, 100 ml diffuser bottle with reed sticks, and custom gift box with realistic paper-shred filling.
- Shading and look development for all three required finishes: matte black, matte white, and frosted glass with realistic liquid refraction, wooden grain texture, and packaging paper tactile properties.
- Setting up the required camera angles and compositions (candle and diffuser together, open box layout with paper-shred and sticks, closed box, and presentation shots for each finish).

### Project Details & Terms:
- **Fixed Project Price:** $550 for the entire set of required compositions across all 3 finish variations.
- **Estimated Delivery Time:** 4 to 5 business days for initial preview drafts; final high-resolution renders within 1-2 days following your feedback.
- **Revisions Included:** 3 rounds of revisions to fine-tune camera framing, lighting, materials, and label placements.
- **Deliverables & Rights:** Full commercial usage rights and all editable source files (3D models, textures, scene files, and high-resolution layered renders) are included upon completion.

I am available to start immediately once you share the reference images, dimensions, and logo files. I would love to discuss any specific details or camera requirements you have in mind.
