Hi,

I have over 9 years of experience in full CG movie production, real-time visualization, and procedural workflows, primarily working at a lead/supervisor level.

I have a very sharp eye for lighting and mood, having lit and composited over 300 production shots. I designed studio-wide Maya and Nuke workflows, and authored master Nuke templates that standardized our compositing across multiple large-scale cinematic attractions. While I have successfully managed teams of lighting artists (7+), I strongly prefer hands-on technical work over people management, and I do not enjoy tasks like planning, bidding, or interfacing with stakeholders and investors.

In my lead roles, I have collaborated closely with Technical Directors to build robust CGI movie production pipelines, and with Art Directors to ensure our visuals align perfectly with the creative direction of the movie. I also bring hands-on experience using AI—not for content generation, but specifically for pipeline automation, optimization, and improving my personal workflows. Additionally, you can view my [Unfinished Project Reel](https://youtu.be/3gb4lI7_XRA), it's project that was paused.

I'm currently based in Krakow, Poland. I am also open to relocation depending on the relocation package. I am currently available and interested to discuss the position in more detail. I would love to continue this conversation so we can figure out which of my skills you need the most and how I can best contribute to your team.

Cheers,  
Oleh
