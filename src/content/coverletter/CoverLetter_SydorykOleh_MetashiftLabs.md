Hi,

I have over 9 years of experience in full CG movie production, real-time visualization, and procedural workflows, primarily working at a lead/supervisor level. 

At my previous position, I led the transition of our traditional rendering pipeline to Unreal Engine 5. We produced multiple attractions and movies entirely in Unreal. I was responsible for the lighting workflow, building procedural environments using PCG, and creating complex stylized FX with Niagara. Here is an example of an Unreal project where I handled both the FX and cinematic lighting: https://sydoryk.com/detail/SunnyBunny

Beyond art, I have deep experience in IT, pipeline automation, and DevOps for creative studios. I've engineered custom Python pipelines for cross-network file validation, automated asset workflows, and managed workstation lifecycles. I rely heavily on a procedural mindset to automate and scale up asset production, which helps me bridge the gap between art direction and technical constraints.

While I might not have direct commercial experience with Unity or low-poly top-down MMOs, I learn quickly and hope you can see my deep foundation in real-time engines, lighting, and procedural tools as a strong bridge to this role. I can easily switch to Unity as needed. Given my background in Python and pipeline automation, I am very comfortable setting up workflow standards, building shaders, working in agile sprints, and yes, I've even experimented with game automation before.

I'm currently based in Krakow, Poland, so the occasional meetings here work perfectly for me. I also have a strong meme game and I've played my fair share of MMOs. I would love to continue this conversation so we can figure out which of my skills you need the most and how I can best contribute to your team.

Cheers,  
Oleh
