I have extensive experience placing products into real photographic environments, which perfectly matches your need to integrate your premium kitchen object into real kitchen interiors. 

Since you will provide the product images, dimensions, and interior backgrounds, we can jump straight into production. I'm interested in building a long-term working relationship and not just a one-off project, so I am very open to ongoing work as you develop new imagery and colourways.

### Proposed Workflow

- **3D Modeling & Rendering**: I will create a highly accurate 3D model of your kitchen product and render it in high quality.
- **Environment Setup**: I will build a simple scene in Houdini to match the lighting and camera angle of your provided interior images.
- **Compositing**: Using Nuke, the industry standard tool for image compositing, I will seamlessly integrate the rendered product into your background images. This allows for perfect compositing, including proper contact shadows and accurate reflections on the counter to make it look naturally photographed.
- **Review & Feedback**: We will refine the scale, materials, and lighting to ensure photorealism.

### Pricing

My pricing ranges from **$100 to $400 per product**. The final price depends on the complexity of the 3D model and how many final images require compositing.

I am currently available and interested to discuss the project in more detail.

### Answers to Screening Questions

**Describe your recent experience with similar projects**
I have extensive experience modeling products and placing them into photographic environments. Recently, I've worked on high-quality product visualizations where I matched lighting and camera angles using Houdini, and seamlessly integrated the renders into real backgrounds using Nuke. This workflow ensures physically accurate reflections and proper contact shadows, making the final image look completely natural.

**Please list any certifications related to this project**
While I don't hold traditional certifications, I have years of hands-on, professional experience using industry-standard VFX and compositing tools like Houdini and Nuke. My portfolio demonstrates the high-end quality and realism of my work, which is the most critical requirement for this type of product visualization. My experience: [https://sydoryk.com/resume](https://sydoryk.com/resume)

**How do you collaborate with all the parties on a design/construction project?**
I prefer clear, direct, and visual communication. I can easily work with your provided materials (product images, dimensions, interior references). We can use Upwork, slack, email, or your preferred project management tool to review work-in-progress renders, discuss styling direction, and ensure we're perfectly aligned on the visual goals before moving to final delivery.

**What experience do you have working with different project types?**
My background spans a wide variety of 3D projects, from highly detailed industrial equipment and apparel visualization to stylized product renders and short CGI movies. This versatility allows me to adapt quickly to different aesthetic requirements, meaning I can easily achieve the premium, high-end look you want for your kitchen objects.

**How do you incorporate feedback and changes into your work?**
I typically provide a work-in-progress (WIP) render early on to ensure the camera angle, lighting, and scale match your vision before committing to the final high-resolution render. Because my Houdini and Nuke workflow is highly procedural, making adjustments to materials, lighting, or compositing based on your feedback is fast and non-destructive.
