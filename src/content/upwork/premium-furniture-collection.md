### Technical Approach & Workflow

While your post mentions 3ds Max and Corona, my pipeline is built around **Houdini and Karma (Solaris)**. This is a comparable, state-of-the-art Hollywood-grade pipeline that excels at handling complex shading, physically accurate lighting, and large-scale rendering with ultimate flexibility. It perfectly meets (and often exceeds) the quality standard required for high-end furniture visualization.

Here is a brief overview of my workflow:

1. **Modeling & Preparation:** I carefully import and optimize your CAD/blueprint files. If necessary, I reconstruct them into clean, quad-dominant meshes, adding essential high-end details like customized stitching, realistic piping, and wrinkles. For upholstery, I simulate cloth draping using Houdini Vellum to achieve beautiful, natural fabric folds.
2. **Material Creation (Lookdev in Solaris):** This is where the magic happens. I utilize MaterialX within Solaris to create highly detailed, physically accurate shaders. For fabrics like bouclé and linen, I employ complex displacement setups and micro-geometry to capture their unique tactile qualities. For woods and metals, I focus on realistic roughness breakups and subtle imperfections to make them believable.
3. **Studio Setup & Batch Automation:** For the e-commerce renders, I create a dedicated, physically accurate studio lighting rig. Since you need a batch of products, I leverage Houdini's automation capabilities to manage camera framing and lighting scripts, guaranteeing perfect consistency across the entire collection (I recently used this exact workflow to produce over 7,000 unique furniture variants).
4. **Lifestyle Renders:** While my primary focus is often product-centric, I have access to a comprehensive, high-quality 3D asset library that allows me to quickly and effectively assemble any architectural interior space needed to complement your furniture. I use cinematic lighting techniques - balancing physically based sun/sky systems with interior accents - to evoke a specific mood and highlight the premium nature of the collection.
5. **Rendering & Post-Production:** I render out high-resolution images using Karma XPU for incredible speed and photorealistic quality. Finally, I use **Nuke** for advanced compositing and post-production, applying subtle color grading and fine-tuning to ensure a flawless, commercial-grade final result.
