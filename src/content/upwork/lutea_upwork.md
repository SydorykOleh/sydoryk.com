Hi there,

With extensive experience as a 3D Generalist and Motion Designer, I specialize in creating high-end, cinematic product animations that convey technical innovation with a premium, luxury aesthetic. Your goal to blend an Apple product launch vibe with clean biotech visualization perfectly aligns with my expertise in procedural materials, premium studio lighting, and elegant motion design.

### Proposed Workflow

1. **Look Development & Lighting:** We'll start by establishing the "clean, clinical, futuristic" visual tone. I'll set up a premium studio lighting environment and develop the high-end materials for the TENCEL™ outer layer, Graphene performance layer, and Fascera™ recovery layer, ensuring they look soft and luxurious rather than harsh or industrial.
2. **Hero Still Render:** I'll construct and render the high-resolution exploded/layered view of the product, locking in the premium aesthetic and lighting before we move to motion.
3. **Layer Breakdown Animation (9–15s):** I'll animate a smooth, elegant reveal of each layer. We'll integrate minimal, clinical UI typography to label each component seamlessly, maintaining that calm, editorial feel.
4. **Delivery & Cutdowns:** Once the master animation is approved, I'll deliver the high-quality files and reformat the sequence into the requested social media formats (1x 9:16 TikTok, 1x 9:16 Instagram Reel), ensuring the pacing is optimized for mobile viewing.

### Software & Technical Approach

I will use **Houdini** to achieve the intricate textile architecture and smooth, procedural reveals. Rendering will be done to ensure the "Bio-Luxury" materials look photorealistic, soft, and premium, avoiding any cartoonish or overly mechanical sci-fi looks. Nuke will be used for compositing the subtle scientific UI overlays.

**Timeline:** 10-14 days for the first deliverable.
**Budget:** Let's discuss to align with your exact scope and expectations.

Looking forward to discussing the project further.

Best regards,  
Oleh Sydoryk
