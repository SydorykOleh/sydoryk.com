Hi there,

I am a 3D artist specializing in photorealistic product visualization and automated render pipelines. Your project for **Night Game** is perfectly aligned with my expertise in building robust 3D models and rendering multiple color, lens, and angle variations from a single source of truth.

### Proposed Workflow

1.  **3D Modeling & Topology:** I will model the two frame styles (sport shield and chunky aviator) to exact kids' proportions. The topology will be meticulously optimized so that materials can be cleanly assigned and swapped.
2.  **Look Development & Texturing:** I will create photorealistic materials for the 4 frame colorways and 2 lens tints, ensuring they look indistinguishable from real photography.
3.  **Procedural Rendering Pipeline:** Instead of rendering each combination manually, I will set up a procedural workflow in Houdini. This ensures that every variation (colorways, lenses, hero angles, backgrounds) is rendered with absolute consistency. If you need design changes later, we can update the core model and automatically re-render all variations.
4.  **Turntable Animation:** I will produce a smooth, high-quality turntable MP4 sequence for your AI ad usage.
5.  **Final Delivery:** You will receive the rendered images (web seamless, ad navy, transparent PNGs), the turntable videos, and the perfectly organized editable source files so you have full control over your assets.

### Relevant Experience
I have extensive experience creating 3D product configurators and batch-rendering systems for apparel, electronics, and accessories. This means I understand the critical importance of consistency when rendering multiple SKUs for pre-order campaigns. You can see some relevant examples of similar configurator and batch-rendered product work below.

Let's discuss how we can make Night Game's launch visually stunning.

Best regards,  
Oleh
