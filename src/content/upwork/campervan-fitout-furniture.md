I have extensive experience translating technical CAD designs and furniture joinery into photorealistic product renders, packaging visualizations, and clear 3D assembly/fitout animations. 

Your campervan modular furniture project involves both precision technical communication (drawings, packaging in a box, structured script) and high-impact marketing visuals (photorealistic unit renders, multi-unit combinations, and animated showcase videos).

---

### File Request & Initial Step

> **Note on Project Attachments**: As there were no files or drawings attached to your job post, please feel free to share your existing CAD models (STEP, IGES, SolidWorks, Rhino, or Fusion 360), 2D technical drawings, packaging dimensions, or reference sketches. Once reviewed, I can confirm exact timeline milestones and provide a tailored fixed quote.

---

### Proposed Workflow

1. **CAD Ingestion & Packaging Analysis**:
   - Import and inspect your CAD geometry, verifying structural dimensions, panel thicknesses (e.g. lightweight plywood, birch multiplex, aluminum extrusions), hardware fittings, fasteners, and locking mechanisms.
   - Model and stage the **flat-pack packaging in a box**, showing realistic corrugated cardboard, protective inserts, labeling, and organized component packing.

2. **Assembly & Presentation Scripting**:
   - Structure a clear step-by-step narrative/script for both the technical assembly guide and the promotional presentation.
   - Outline key camera movements, callouts, and feature highlights (e.g., quick-release brackets, drawer slides, convertible bench/bed transitions, storage compartments) before kicking off full rendering.

3. **Material Lookdev & Studio / Van Environment**:
   - Build physically calibrated PBR shaders in Houdini (Solaris / Karma) for wood grain, laminated textures, anodized aluminum, powder-coated steel, and fabric cushions.
   - Create a clean studio lighting setup for catalog visuals as well as a realistic campervan interior environment to show true-to-life scale, fitment, and natural ambient lighting.

4. **Photorealistic Renders (Single Unit & Modular Combinations)**:
   - **Single Unit Renders**: Hero angle, open/closed drawer states, joinery close-ups, and the packaged unit in its box.
   - **Modular Combinations (3.1)**: Staging 2+ modular combinations inside the campervan space, demonstrating how individual units interlock, adapt, and maximize van interior utility.

5. **3D Animation & Video Production**:
   - **Unit Video**: Dynamic showcase highlighting build quality, transformation mechanisms, and packaging-to-product reveal.
   - **Combination Videos (2 Layouts)**: Seamless animated transitions demonstrating the 2 different modular configurations installed in the vehicle, clearly explaining ease of fitout and versatility.

6. **Post-Production & Delivery**:
   - Multi-pass compositing, color correction, motion blur, and technical callout graphics in Nuke / DaVinci Resolve.
   - Delivery of high-resolution stills (print & e-commerce ready) and high-bitrate video exports (4K / 1080p 60fps MP4/ProRes).

---

### Technical Capabilities & Software Pipeline

- **3D Pipeline**: SideFX Houdini (Solaris USD & Karma raytracer) for procedural modular rigging and Hollywood-grade rendering.
- **CAD Interoperability**: Direct support for STEP, IGES, SolidWorks, Rhino, and Parasolid formats with clean quad-dominant surface meshing.
- **Compositing & Motion Graphics**: Foundry Nuke & DaVinci Resolve for precision color grading, lighting tweaks, and technical overlays.
