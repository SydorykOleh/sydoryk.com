I have extensive experience creating hyper-realistic visualizations, building automated colorway pipelines, and preparing assets for interactive web configurators. For Kindred Motorworks' bespoke Bronco builds, I can deliver crisp, studio-grade imagery across every model, color code, and custom body option with complete visual consistency.

### Proposed Workflow

- **CAD Ingestion & Mesh Preparation**: Ingest your native 3D CAD models (STEP, IGES, Rhino, SolidWorks, or Alias). Tessellate the NURBS surfaces into clean, high-density geometry with seamless body panel curvature, precise shut lines, and authentic bevels.
- **Automated Colorways & Shader Library**: Set up procedural automotive shaders matching your exact OEM/custom color codes, including multi-coat metallic fleck, clearcoat depth, satin finishes, chrome trim, textured plastics, and interior leather/stitching.
- **Studio Lighting & Angle Consistency**: Establish a dedicated virtual lighting rig with softbox reflections tailored to accentuate the Bronco's iconic body lines. Every model and color variant will be rendered from locked camera angles on a unified background for seamless transitions.
- **Interactive 360° & 3D Viewer Options**:
  - **Prerendered 360° Sequences**: Multi-frame raytraced image sequences allowing customers to drag and spin the vehicle smoothly with photorealistic raytraced reflections and instant color switching (see live examples: [Microphone 360 Viewer](/detail/MicConfigurator), [Headphones 360 Viewer](/detail/Headphones)).
  - **Real-Time 3D WebGL Configurator**: Optimized real-time 3D assets for interactive 3D browser inspection with orbit, zoom, and live part customization (see live demo: [3D Fabric Configurator](/detail/FabricConfigurator/)).
- **Post-Processing & Delivery Pipeline**: Utilize Nuke for automated batch compositing, color correction, and alpha channel generation (transparent or solid backdrop) to output optimized, web-ready WebP/JPEG files.

### 3D Asset Availability & Modeling from Reference

- **Do you have 3D CAD models ready?** If you already have 3D CAD files for the Bronco models, hardtop/soft-top variations, wheels, and trim options, we can immediately begin shader calibration and rendering.
- **Custom Modeling from Reference**: If 3D CAD files are not available for certain body modifications or accessories, I can build high-accuracy 3D models from 2D CAD blueprints and photographic reference. Please note that building geometry from scratch will add extra modeling time compared to working directly with existing CAD data.

### Software Stack & Production Pipeline

- **Primary 3D & Rendering**: Houdini (Solaris / Karma) for procedural lookdev, lighting, and batch colorway rendering.
- **Compositing & Post-Production**: Nuke for pixel-level color calibration, reflections balancing, and automated batch output.
- **Real-Time Web 3D (if needed)**: Three.js / WebGL optimization and texture baking.

I am available to start immediately on a pilot test angle or colorway to establish the visual baseline for Kindred Motorworks.
