Hi,

Having delivered over 8,000 standardized product images across more than 200 SKUs for a major furniture project, I am fully equipped to handle your hardware handles and knobs with precision.

### Proposed Workflow and Procedural Setup

To ensure 100% visual consistency across all your 53 SKUs, I use a fully procedural workflow based on **Houdini** and **Nuke** (rather than relying on manual Photoshop adjustments). This allows me to establish a master studio kitchen set where lighting, backgrounds, and drop shadows are perfectly standardized across the entire collection. 

* **Procedural Flexibility:** If we need to tweak lighting, background tones, or shadow intensity at any point, I can simply adjust the master setup and automatically re-render the entire catalog with the new changes applied perfectly to every asset.
* **3D model and Materials:** I can model all the hardware directly in 3D from your media and CAD files. I have extensive experience crafting high-end wood and metal materials (like brushed brass, nickel, and matte black), ensuring the texture and metallic grain are photorealistic, especially for macro closeups. 

### Offer Details

I can provide the sample set for one SKU as a test for **$200**. The initial setup requires the most work, as I will be building out the master procedural environment, setting up the lighting rigs, and ensuring the materials are perfectly dialed in for your specific products. 

Once the master setup is approved and we move forward with the entire catalog, my rate drops to **$25 per SKU**, as the procedural workflow allows for high efficiency.

This rate includes the 6 requested assets per SKU:
- **Hero Studio Shot:** Clean front 3/4 angled view on a light neutral background (cutout/isolated).
- **In-Situ Micro Shot:** Handle mounted on a single cabinet door/drawer front with realistic lighting.
- **In-Situ Lifestyle Macro Shot:** Full kitchen interior showing the handles in a high-end ambient setting.
- **Texture / Detail Close-up:** Macro crop highlighting the metallic grain/finish.
- **2 Additional Angles/Variations** as per your requirements to complete the 6-image pack.

I am ready to create the test set and prove the quality I can bring to Plook Design's catalog.
