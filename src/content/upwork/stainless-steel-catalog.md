I have extensive experience working with engineering STEP files and creating high-end catalog visuals for metal products. I understand that steel requires a specialized approach because steel does not show its own color, it reflects its environment. I build dedicated studio reflection rigs with controlled white and dark reflection cards, softboxes, and precise rim lights to define clean contours and highlight metallic finishes accurately.

For your 22 product families, I can establish a standardized master lighting template in Houdini and Karma, guaranteeing consistent camera elevation, focal lengths (85–120mm), scale, and lighting across every graphic set.

### Proposed Workflow

- **STEP File Ingestion & Geometry Prep:** Importing your STEP files directly into Houdini / Moi3D. I inspect and prepare all CAD geometry, ensure exact engineering tolerances, add micro-bevels on hard edges to catch specular highlights naturally, and organize internal components (ball valves, seals, grip rings, flow paths) for cutaway and exploded views.
- **Master Studio Scene & Reflection Design:** Building a unified master lighting setup with a soft light-gray background, overhead softbox, broad side fills, controlled rim lighting, and custom reflection cards. This ensures sharp, crisp edge definition across all stainless steel surfaces and subtle, natural contact shadows.
- **Standardized Catalog Angle Setup:** Locking in the 6 required views per product family:
  1. Primary three-quarter hero view
  2. Rear three-quarter view showing connections and port details
  3. Straight side / front view for technical layouts
  4. Detail macro close-ups (grip rings, threads, seals)
  5. Exploded view for installation and technology presentations
  6. Cutaway / section view highlighting the internal flow path and components
- **High-Resolution Multi-Pass 16-Bit / 32-Bit Rendering:** Rendering in Houdini / Karma using linear 32-bit/16-bit EXR passes to prevent any color banding. I have handled ultra-high resolution pipelines (including delivering film projects in up to 16k resolution), so 6,000 × 6,000 px 16-bit outputs are standard in my pipeline.
- **Post-Processing & Delivery in Nuke:** Compositing passes in Nuke to output 16-bit uncompressed TIFF archival masters, transparent background PNGs with shadows isolated on separate layers, and optimized sRGB versions for web use.

### Relevant Past Experience

- **CAD / Industrial Equipment:** Converted complex multi-part STEP systems into detailed marketing and technical catalog renders ([F.N. Smith Industrial Equipment](/detail/IndustrialEquipment)).
- **Multi-Family Catalog Consistency:** Built procedural rendering pipelines and master lighting rigs in Houdini Solaris to deliver thousands of consistent catalog visuals across multiple complete furniture collections ([Sofa Collections](/detail/Sofas)).
- **High-Bitrate & Large-Format Delivery:** Extensive background managing high-resolution, multi-pass linear EXR workflows with zero banding for commercial and large-format theater projects ([18k movie](/detail/NaturaVision)).

I am available to start immediately and would love to review a sample STEP file to demonstrate the initial studio setup for your catalog.
