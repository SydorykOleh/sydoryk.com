I've done a lot of work creating dynamic 3D animations and visual effects, particularly with hard-surface models and industrial elements like the metal fabrication vibes you're looking for. Since you need an energetic and eye-catching logo reveal, I'd love to help you bring those welding and cutting themes to life.

### Proposed Workflow

- **Concept & Look Development:** We'll start by locking down the exact "metal" look you want-sparks, glowing edges, and heavy, scratched steel textures.
- **3D Modeling & Materials:** I'll take your vector logo, build it out in full 3D, and apply physically accurate metal shaders, including details like weld seams or heat discoloration.
- **Animation & FX Simulation:** I'll animate the energetic reveal and simulate dynamic elements like flying sparks, heat distortion, and heavy impacts using Houdini and Unreal Engine.
- **Rendering & Compositing:** I will render the sequence with photorealistic lighting and composite the final video so it's perfectly optimized for your new website.

### Screening Questions

**How will you begin this project?**
I always start by discussing the creative vision. For a welding company, we'll decide on the specific visual metaphors-like a laser cutting out the logo, a welding torch fusing the letters together, or heavy metal parts slamming into place. Once the concept is approved, I'll take your vector logo, extrude it into a detailed 3D model, and begin applying realistic metal materials and lighting.

**Do you customize each or just swap out the logo in an After Effects template?**
I build everything custom from scratch in full 3D using software like Houdini, Blender. I do not use After Effects templates. This ensures your logo reveal is 100% unique, allowing for custom camera angles, realistic 3D lighting, and complex physics simulations (like flying sparks or molten metal) that a 2D template simply can't achieve.
