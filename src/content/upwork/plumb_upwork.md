I'm Oleh, a 3D artist specializing in photorealistic product visualization. I've read your brief for Plumb and understand the precise "institutional-aesthetic" you're aiming for—restrained, minimalist, and premium-functional, perfectly aligned with the vibe of Bearaby Napper or Buffy. 

Having delivered campaign-grade 3D renders for successful projects like the [Hush earplugs Kickstarter campaign](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/hush/hush-the-worlds-first-smart-earplug), which raised over $500,000, I know exactly how critical pristine, non-AI-generated imagery is to establishing trust and funding momentum. My workflow guarantees professional, consistent results with meticulous attention to soft-goods materials.

### Proposed Workflow for Round 1
*   **Material Setup**: Accurately replicating the fine-gauge viscose-from-bamboo knit for the sleeve and pillowcase, ensuring a cashmere-weight loungewear feel. I will also accurately model the 2-3 rows of interior silicone grippers, the restrained "PLUMB" embroidery, and the open-cell polyurethane foam texture for the contoured hourglass pillow.
*   **Lighting & Composition**: Setting up restrained, soft natural light from the upper left with subtle shadows to match your requested editorial, premium-functional aesthetic (no glossy plastic or oversaturated AI looks). Images will be delivered in your standard 3:2 aspect ratio.
*   **Rendering**: Delivering 5 high-resolution hero angles (front, side, three-quarter, top-down flatlay, in-bedding context) and 1 modest lifestyle scene.
*   **Revisions**: 2 revision rounds included to ensure every detail aligns perfectly with your vision.
*   **Source Delivery**: Final files provided for full ownership (along with Houdini source files for any cloth simulations).

### Timeline & Next Steps
I can comfortably deliver Round 1 within your 2-3 week timeframe, prioritizing craftsmanship and doing it right the first time over rushed iterations. We can communicate entirely async via written-first channels as you prefer.
