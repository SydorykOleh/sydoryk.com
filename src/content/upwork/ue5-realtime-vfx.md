I have extensive hands-on experience developing real-time visual effects in Unreal Engine 5, combining native Niagara particle systems, FluidNinja (LIVE/VFX), and Houdini-driven procedural simulations to deliver high-fidelity fluid, smoke, energy, and environmental effects. Having delivered multiple cinematic attractions and movies produced directly in Unreal Engine, I focus on creating visually impactful effects that run efficiently and meet tight production deadlines.

---

### Core FX Capabilities & Technical Pipeline

- **Niagara Systems & Real-Time Emitters**: Authoring custom Niagara modules, dynamic particle emitters, ribbon/mesh trails, GPU-driven particles, and event handlers for responsive, high-performance visual effects.
- **Fluid & Smoke Simulations (FluidNinja & Niagara Fluids)**: Practical experience with FluidNinja LIVE and FluidNinja VFX for real-time interactive fluids, reactive fire/smoke, density fields, and baking optimized flipbooks and flow/vector fields directly inside Unreal Engine.
- **Houdini to Unreal Engine Pipeline**: Leveraging SideFX Houdini and Houdini Engine to build complex pyro, smoke, fluid, and destruction simulations. I routinely export simulations into UE5 using Vertex Animation Textures (VAT), flipbooks, VDB/heterogeneous volumes, or optimized Alembic caches for seamless integration.
- **Material & Shader Development**: Crafting performant PBR master materials, custom HLSL functions, depth-fade blending, parallax occlusion, distortion buffers, and emissive dynamics for realistic smoke dissipation, fire, and magical/stylized effects.
- **Unreal Engine 5 Feature Set**: Working with Nanite, Lumen lighting interactions, Niagara Fluids, Substrate materials, and real-time volumetric rendering.

---

### Proposed Workflow

1. **Briefing & Style Alignment**:
   - Review concept art, references, and art direction notes.
   - Establish technical constraints (target framerate, platform targets, cinematic vs. gameplay performance budgets).

2. **Simulation & FX Prototyping**:
   - Build core emitters in Niagara for direct real-time control, or simulate complex fluid/pyro solvers in Houdini.
   - Generate custom noise fields, vector fields, and sprite sheets/flipbooks tailored to the required visual motion.

3. **In-Engine Integration & Lookdev**:
   - Implement shaders, dynamic parameter exposed controls, and lighting response inside UE5.
   - Fine-tune color grading, bloom, motion blur, and particle lifetime curves to achieve photographic realism or stylized impact.

4. **Optimization & Delivery**:
   - Profile draw calls, GPU particle count, and overdraw using Unreal's profiling tools.
   - Package clean, organized assets with clear naming conventions and folder structures.
