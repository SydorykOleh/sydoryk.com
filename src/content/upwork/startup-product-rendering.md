I've helped several startups create high-end 3D product visuals and animations, taking projects from reference materials all the way to final photorealistic renders and environmental scenes. As an independent artist, I love experimenting with new ideas and concepts. Since you are looking for a long-term partner who can also contribute creatively to mood boards and branding concepts, my experience building complete 3D scenes for products aligns well with your needs.

### Proposed Workflow

- **Review and Concept**: Review your inspiration materials, references, and specifications for the new collection.
- **3D Modeling**: Build highly accurate 3D models of the products.
- **Environment & Lighting**: Develop creative environments and lighting setups to highlight product features and storytelling.
- **Rendering**: Render high-resolution photorealistic images for web, print, and social media.
- **Animation**: Produce animations and motion content as required for marketing campaigns.
- **Delivery**: Deliver all layered PSDs and approved source files (Cinema 4D/Blender/Houdini) upon completion.

### Pricing

My hourly rate is **$45/hr**. I am also open to discussing a monthly retainer based on the 5–10 product renderings required per month once we define the specific scope per product.

I am currently available and interested to discuss the project and your upcoming collections in more detail.
