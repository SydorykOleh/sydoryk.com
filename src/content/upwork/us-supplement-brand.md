I have extensive experience creating premium, true 3D photorealistic product renders that emphasize depth, material properties, and authentic physical details. I understand exactly what you need to elevate your brand imagery to the "Seed" standard: real-world geometric accuracy for the jar and cap, physically accurate lighting and reflections, and a perfectly wrapped label with subtle edge lift rather than a flat Photoshop mockup.

### Proposed Workflow

- **Reference & Discovery:** Review your current photography, label artwork, and dimensions. I'll analyze the Seed and Sports Research examples to match the exact low camera angle, soft lighting, and premium feel.
- **Precision 3D Modeling:** Build the exact geometry of your jar from scratch. This includes the subtle domed top and rounded edge of the lid, the soft inset foot and curved bottom edge of the jar, ensuring the model possesses real physical weight and volume.
- **Material & Texture Development:** 
  - Create the signature cobalt blue plastic with a soft, premium sheen.
  - Develop the smooth white plastic lid (providing both a matte and a gloss option for your review).
  - Apply the label artwork using proper cylindrical mapping, mimicking real-world wrap distortion and subtle paper edge lift.
  - Design three options for the "Made in USA" badge (incorporating the flag-stamp and corner ribbon styles).
- **Lighting & Camera Setup:** Establish a premium studio lighting environment. I'll position the camera slightly below eye level to capture the top of the lid and convey depth. Soft shadows and a subtle base reflection will be dialed in to create that high-end look.
- **Hero Render Delivery:** Produce the main product shot. Minimum 2000x2000px, delivered as a JPEG on pure white (RGB 255,255,255) for Amazon, and a transparent PNG for versatile website/ad use.
- **Lifestyle Scene Integration:** Set up the kitchen marble counter scene. I will simulate the action of unflavored powder pouring into a clear glass of water, capturing the stream and clouding effect mid-pour, alongside the jar and resting lid.
- **Review & Refinement:** Present initial drafts and complete up to two rounds of revisions based on your feedback to ensure perfection.

### Pricing and Answers to Screening Questions

I am comfortable working within your $700 USD fixed-price budget for these deliverables and can complete this within 4-5 business days. 

**Screening Questions:**

**Please show 2 examples of supplement products you've rendered.**
While I have extensive product rendering experience (see examples below like the Mic configurator and Ear Plug packaging), I approach all packaging with the exact same focus on precise material properties (glass, PET, HDPE) and photorealistic label application.

**Please show 2 lifestyle renders you've done.**
My portfolio includes high-end lifestyle staging, like the detailed environment in my Solar Panels project. I should also mention that most of my projects are created using premium studio backgrounds to emphasize product details and lighting.

**Do you build from a physical product sample, or from photos + a label artwork file?**
I typically build highly accurate 3D models from precise photos, dimensions, and label artwork files. This is usually faster and more efficient, though physical samples can be helpful for highly complex bespoke shapes.

**Cost to render a 2nd product later, reusing this exact same 3D model (e.g. same jar, different label)?**
For a simple label swap and re-render of the existing model and scenes, the cost would be approximately $100 per product.

**Cost to build a new model for a different-shaped bottle?**
To build a new geometric model from scratch, set up materials, and execute the same hero and lifestyle scenes, the cost would be roughly $200-$300 depending on the complexity of the new bottle design.

I'm available to begin immediately and would love to start testing the lid materials for you.
