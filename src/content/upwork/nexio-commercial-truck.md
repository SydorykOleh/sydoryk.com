I have extensive experience building scalable, hyper-realistic 3D visualization platforms from engineered CAD models, which makes this commercial cab-over truck project a great fit. Since you already have the existing Nexio cab-over and chassis CAD files, we can immediately begin developing a render-ready, hyper-realistic master platform designed for modular vocational integration.

### Proposed Workflow

- **Master Chassis Optimization:** I will import the Nexio CAD models into Houdini, clean and optimize the geometry for rendering, and ensure accurate wheelbase and proportional stance without the burden of unnecessary engineering tolerances.
- **Modular Vocational Integration:** Using Houdini and Solaris, I will create a flexible, repeatable framework to ingest and adapt purchased vocational body models (e.g., tow trucks, box trucks, sweepers). This system will handle realistic mounting alignment, scale adjustments, and geometry modifications for believable fitment across all variations.
- **Industrial Look Development:** I will author highly realistic, physically based materials (steel, aluminum, composites, OEM paint finishes) ensuring commercial automotive campaign-level quality. The focus will be on authentic surface behaviors, true-to-life reflections, and accurate vehicle mass cues like tire deformation and suspension compression.
- **Standardized Rendering Pipeline:** I will establish a repeatable lighting and rendering template using Karma, featuring a neutral studio-style environment, consistent camera lenses, and locked framing. This guarantees uniform high-resolution outputs for all required angles (Front, Rear, Profile, 3/4s, Top-down) across dozens of configurations. For context on my capability to handle massive permutations, I recently built a pipeline for a modular sofa project that successfully delivered over 8,000 isolated product renderings in consistent style and quality across multiple collections.
- **Delivery & Iteration:** I will deliver the standardized set of isolated truck renderings for your commercial marketing materials, ensuring consistent, photorealistic results that mirror OEM marketing photography, and refine details based on your feedback.

I am available to start immediately and would love to discuss the details of your preferred vocational bodies and timeline.
