Hi there,

I am a highly experienced 3D artist specializing in luxury product visualization, with direct experience in creating high-end, photorealistic renders and animations for fine jewelry. Since your 3D models for the 6 pieces are already prepared, I can dive straight into look development, precise material refinement, and lighting to achieve the clean editorial presentation you are looking for.

### Proposed Workflow

1. **Material & Lighting Setup**: Establish an elegant lighting environment and meticulously craft the materials (gold, silver, diamonds, etc.) to ensure complete physical accuracy and a premium look.
2. **Still Renders**: Produce the full 3D renders on a black background for all 6 jewelry pieces, focusing on stunning reflections and a luxury feel.
3. **Rotating Shots**: Create the short rotating product shots (1-2 seconds) tailored to the specific counts for each piece.
4. **Teaser Videos & Assembly Animations**: Assemble and edit the 3 collection teaser videos, the individual product videos, and precisely orchestrate the 2x assembly/construction-style animations for each piece.
5. **Review & Revisions**: Ensure all deliverables perfectly match your creative vision and brand aesthetic.

### Why Choose Me?

- **Jewelry Expertise**: I have extensive experience working with intricate jewelry models, ensuring that gems and metals catch the light perfectly to deliver the high-end results luxury brands demand.
- **High-End Delivery**: I pride myself on delivering clean, premium results that elevate luxury brands, avoiding stylized or environment-heavy scenes when clean realism is the goal.
- **Efficient Pipeline**: My workflow is optimized for producing multiple variations and animations consistently across a collection, ensuring all video elements are perfectly cohesive.

I would love to discuss the specific aesthetic you are aiming for and review the existing models to give you a precise timeline. Please review my relevant jewelry and high-end configurator work below.
