I have extensive experience converting 2D engineering diagrams, CAD schematics, and technical drawings into photorealistic 3D renderings and animated sequences. Visualizing complex electrical systems, power flows, and mechanical assemblies so that both technical engineers and sales clients understand them clearly is something I do regularly.

### Proposed Workflow

- **Review & Technical Breakdown:** We will review your 2D schematics, system architecture diagrams, and engineering references to map out all components, power paths, and operational stages.
- **3D Modeling & CAD Layout:** I will build and refine the 3D assets in Houdini, ensuring technical accuracy and proper scale across all mechanical and electrical parts.
- **Materials, Shading & Lighting:** Applying clean, physically accurate materials (enclosures, wiring, metal finishes, circuit boards) and modern studio lighting for a crisp, high-end technical look.
- **Animation & Power Flow FX:** Developing animated sequences, exploded views, and directional power flow / system architecture effects to show product operation clearly.
- **Compositing & Motion Graphics:** Finalizing render passes in After Effects and Nuke with clean graphic overlays, callouts, and deliverable formats for your website, presentations, and whitepapers.
- **Review & Fast Iteration:** Collaborating directly with your engineering and marketing teams to implement technical feedback quickly.

**Technical & Industrial Portfolio Highlights:**  
- [Industrial Equipment](/detail/IndustrialEquipment) - Modeled, shaded, and rendered complex multi-component machinery and internal mechanical assemblies.
- [Solar Panels Configurator](/detail/SolarPanels) - Interactive 3D technical visualization of renewable energy hardware and power infrastructure.
- [Conservatory](/detail/Conservatory) - Time-lapse structural assembly animation showcasing detailed construction and materials.
