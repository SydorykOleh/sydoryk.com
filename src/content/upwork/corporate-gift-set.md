I have extensive experience creating photorealistic 3D product visuals and packaging renders from technical specifications and supplier references. For your corporate gift set, I will build accurate 3D models matching your exact dimensions and establish tactile look development for all three finish variations (matte black, matte white, frosted glass), including realistic paper-shred filling simulations and wooden textures.

### Proposed Workflow

- **Dimension & Shape Modeling**: Build precision 3D models for the 180 g scented candle jar with wooden lid, 100 ml reed diffuser bottle, reed sticks, and custom rigid gift box based on your exact measurements and supplier reference images.
- **Material & Look Development**: Craft high-fidelity shaders for all three required finishes:
  - **Matte Black**: Rich, micro-textured diffuse absorption with subtle edge highlights.
  - **Matte White**: Soft, clean satin diffusion with natural ambient scattering.
  - **Frosted Glass**: Realistic translucent glass refraction, depth diffusion, and interior liquid interaction.
  - **Accents & Packaging**: Realistic wood grain detailing on lids, natural porous reed sticks, high-end box textures with foil/print logo placement, and dynamic paper-shred filler volume.
- **Staging & Camera Compositions**: Set up the required camera views and studio lighting for each material finish:
  - Candle and diffuser together outside the box.
  - Open gift box with both products nested in paper-shred.
  - Reed sticks arranged horizontally above the products.
  - Closed gift box hero shot.
  - Presentation compositions tailored for website and marketing materials.
- **Review & Revisions**: Share clay and low-noise preview renders to verify framing, scale, materials, and label alignment before final high-resolution output.
- **Final High-Resolution Output**: Render pristine, publication-ready visuals optimized for web, presentations, and print, along with complete source files.

### Project Terms & Deliverables

- **Fixed Project Price**: **$800** (covers all modeling, lookdev for 3 finishes, paper-shred setup, and all required composition renders).
- **Estimated Delivery Time**: **6-8 business days** for initial draft previews; final high-resolution renders delivered within **1-2 business days** upon feedback approval.
- **Revisions Included**: **2 rounds of revisions** included to fine-tune camera angles, lighting intensity, shader properties, and label placement.
- **Source Files & Rights**: **Full commercial usage rights** and all editable source files (3D models, textures, scene files, and layered high-resolution renders) are 100% included upon completion.

I am available to start immediately once you share the reference images, dimensions, and logo files. I look forward to bringing this gift set to life with photorealistic detail.
