Creating realistic, technically credible, and premium visual assets is exactly my expertise. I specialize in turning complex product differences—like the structural and sealing advantages of your Permacure repair vs. traditional string repairs—into clear, engaging visual explanations.

### Proposed Workflow
- **Initial Concept/Sketch:** (Days 1–3) I will start by reviewing your provided reference images and 3D cutaway examples. I'll create an initial layout showing the side-by-side comparison (Traditional vs. Permacure) to ensure the framing, scale, and cross-section details are accurate.
- **First Polished Draft:** (Days 4–6) I'll proceed to the high-end rendering phase, ensuring a realistic, clean, and professional style. The traditional repair will visually communicate a "forced, less integrated" look, while the Permacure side will clearly show it bonding, filling the injury path, and looking built-to-hold.
- **Final Files Delivery:** (Within 1-2 weeks) I will deliver layered high-resolution files (Photoshop/PSD), along with print-ready and web-ready PNG/JPGs, keeping all text/callouts on separate layers for your flexibility across different marketing channels.

I'm confident I can help you effectively communicate the advanced nature of the Permacure solution to your customers and dealers.
