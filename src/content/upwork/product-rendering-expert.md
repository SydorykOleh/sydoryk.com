I have extensive experience crafting photorealistic 3D product renders tailored for both high-resolution print media and crisp online/e-commerce use. Whether working from existing CAD files, 3D assets, or 2D concept sketches and reference dimensions, I collaborate closely with design teams to bring product concepts to life while adhering strictly to brand guidelines.

### Proposed Workflow

- **Asset Ingestion & Preparation**: Review your CAD data, 3D models, or design concept sheets. Clean up geometry, optimize topology, and set up accurate real-world scale and proportions.
- **Look Development & Shading**: Create physically accurate shaders and textures (metals, plastics, glass, fabrics, packaging labels) that match your brand identity under controlled studio lighting.
- **Lighting & Camera Composition**: Set up hero camera angles and studio lighting rigs optimized for specific media formats (wide hero banners, square e-commerce formats, high-DPI print crops).
- **High-Resolution Rendering & Passes**: Deliver ultra high-resolution renders suitable for print (300+ DPI, clean alpha channels, material ID passes, depth passes) and web-optimized deliverables.
- **Team Collaboration & Revisions**: Share work-in-progress drafts early to align with your design team on framing, finishes, and lighting before final master deliveries.

### Why Choose My Workflow?

- **Dual-Media Optimization**: I ensure that colors, contrast, and resolution are calibrated properly for both digital RGB screens and high-resolution print layouts.
- **Collaborative & Iterative**: Using a procedural Houdini/Solaris/Karma pipeline, adjusting materials, colors, label graphics, or lighting setups based on design team feedback is fast and non-destructive.
- **Brand Consistency**: I set up reproducible lighting environments and color management so your entire product catalog maintains a cohesive visual identity across campaigns.

I am currently available and would love to discuss your upcoming products and media requirements in more detail.
