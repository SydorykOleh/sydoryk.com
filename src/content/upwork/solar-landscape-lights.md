### Introduction

Hi there!  I have extensive experience in CAD conversion and photorealistic rendering, and I can match the realistic materials and lighting styles outlined in your PowerPoint.

### Proposed Workflow & Houdini Advantage

Since you have multiple models (starting with 1 test and scaling up to 15), consistency across all images is key. By using **Houdini** for this project, I can create a fully procedural lighting and shading setup. This means:
- **Consistent Style:** Every model will have the exact same high-quality look, lighting, and materials.
- **Efficiency:** Once the initial setup is approved, applying the same environment and materials to the remaining 14 models will be incredibly fast and perfectly uniform.
- **Lighting Variations:** Creating the "lights ON" vs "lights OFF" versions for both angles will be seamlessly integrated into the pipeline.

### Pricing & Deliverables

Here is my pricing structure for your project:

- **Test Phase (1 Light):** **$100**
  - Includes processing the STP file.
  - Setup of realistic materials based on your PowerPoint.
  - 2 distinct angles.
  - Both "Lights ON" and "Lights OFF" variations.
- **Production Phase (Remaining Models):** **$50 per model**
  - Includes processing the STP file for each model.
  - 2 distinct angles per model.
  - Both "Lights ON" and "Lights OFF" variations.
