Hi there,

I am a senior compositor with extensive experience in Nuke, and I would love to help you achieve the stylized cinematic look for your 3D animation VFX project.

Having worked as a Lead Compositing Artist on numerous animated projects and short movies, I specialize in blending 3D rendered passes (including those from Houdini) into a cohesive, highly stylized final look. Taking inspiration from 2D anime and translating that energy and style into 3D is a fantastic creative challenge that I am very well-prepared for. 

### Proposed Workflow
1. **Look Development & Reference Analysis:** Reviewing your Houdini renders alongside the 2D anime fight scene references to establish the desired stylized realism and cinematic language.
2. **Nuke Compositing Setup:** Building a robust compositing template in Nuke to handle the Houdini AOV passes efficiently.
3. **Stylization & Effects:** Applying stylized treatments, color grading, glow, motion blur, and integrating VFX elements to match the intense anime aesthetic.
4. **Refinement & Delivery:** Providing iterations for feedback and ensuring the final cinematic look perfectly aligns with your vision.

To answer your question regarding related work, I have included examples of my compositing and lighting work on animated movies below. My Showreel specifically showcases a variety of sequences where cinematic lighting and compositing were crucial.

Let's discuss the specifics of your scenes and how we can elevate the Houdini renders to the next level.

Best regards,
