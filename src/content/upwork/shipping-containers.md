I have extensive experience creating photorealistic, catalog-ready product visualizations, which is exactly what you need for a high-quality shipping container image library. I understand that these renders must be indistinguishable from real manufacturer photography, maintaining perfect consistency in lighting, angles, and proportions across all container variations.

### Proposed Workflow

1. **3D Modeling:** I will create highly accurate 3D models of the 20', 40', High Cube, and specialized containers based on reference materials and dimensions.
2. **Texturing and Shading:** I'll apply realistic materials, including subtle imperfections like standard wear, paint textures, and realistic metal finishes to ensure they look like actual products rather than generic CG assets.
3. **Lighting and Studio Setup:** I will establish a consistent virtual studio lighting rig. This ensures that the front views, angled perspectives, and interior shots match perfectly across the entire catalog.
4. **Rendering:** I'll render all required variations (doors open/closed, white background, transparent PNGs) using a robust pipeline to guarantee efficiency and consistency as your project expands.
5. **Review and Final Delivery:** After your feedback, I'll deliver the final photorealistic renders along with the editable source files and full commercial usage rights.

I use industry-standard software (Houdini and Karma) for rendering, which is exceptionally well-suited for managing large, consistent asset libraries.
### Estimated Timeline, Budget & Deliverables

- **Estimated Timeline:** Typically, creating the highly accurate master models, establishing the virtual studio lighting, and rendering the initial batch of variations (20', 40', High Cube, etc.) takes about **1.5 to 2 weeks**.
- **Estimated Budget:** I am open to discussing a fixed project rate based on the exact scope, but as a rough estimate, I usually structure this around $200 per unique container type (which includes all required render views: front, angled, doors open/closed, white background, transparent PNG).
- **Source Files Included:** Yes, I confirm that all editable 3D source files will be included alongside the final renders, transferring full commercial usage rights to you upon completion.

Let me know when you're available to chat to refine these details!
