Hi!

I noticed your project for creating a professional roofing sales presentation and I'm very interested. I specialize in high-end, realistic 3D marketing graphics and have extensive experience creating step-by-step assembly visualizations and cutaway illustrations.

Below are the details on how I would approach your project, my software pipeline, and relevant portfolio examples.

### 1. Project Understanding & Approach

The goal is to create premium printed sales binders featuring a large residential home rendered in 3D. We will show the roofing system built layer by layer:

1. Roof Decking
2. Starter Strips
3. Ice & Water Shield
4. Synthetic Underlayment
5. Architectural Shingles
6. Ridge Vent System
7. Ridge Caps

My approach is to model a highly detailed residential home and meticulously build out the roofing components as separate, precise layers. I will ensure the lighting, texturing, and camera angles highlight the premium quality of the materials, avoiding a "blueprint" look and instead delivering a photorealistic, high-end marketing aesthetic similar to GAF and Owens Corning materials.

### 2. Software Stack & Rendering

**Software Stack:** Houdini and its procedural pipeline, with Nuke for image processing.
**Renderer:** Karma

*Why it fits your project:* Using a procedural workflow allows me to build the layers systematically. If a change is needed (e.g., adjusting the shingle color or the pitch of the roof), the entire sequence of layers can be updated easily. For the final presentation, Nuke ensures clean, artifact-free renders with perfect studio lighting.
