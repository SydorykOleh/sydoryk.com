### How My Pipeline Aligns With Your Workflow

- **Note on 3ds Max & V-Ray:** While my primary production pipeline is built around **Houdini (Solaris / Karma XPU)** rather than 3ds Max and V-Ray, it is a Hollywood-grade USD/MaterialX pipeline that delivers the exact same commercial photorealism - with added advantages for non-destructive lookdev, multi-SKU consistency, and integrated fluid/cloth/particle simulations.
- **CAD Ingestion (`.STEP`, `.SLDPRT`, etc.):** Converting engineering CAD assemblies into clean, artifact-free render geometry with accurate real-world scale, smooth normals, and realistic micro-bevels.
- **Packaging from 2D Dielines:** Building structurally accurate 3D packaging directly from 2D dieline artwork - ensuring precise fold geometry, substrate textures (matte/gloss paperboard, foil, glass, PET/HDPE), and crisp label placement.
- **Soft-Surface & Simulations (Bonus Capabilities):**
  - **Soft-Surface Modeling & Cloth:** Upholstered furniture, cushions, stitching, piping, and natural fabric draping using Houdini Vellum.
  - **VFX & Simulations:** Fluids, splashes, particles, smoke, and cloth dynamics integrated seamlessly into product hero shots and commercial animations.
- **Post-Production & Delivery:** Clean multi-pass EXR/PNG outputs, color-calibrated retouching and compositing in **Nuke**.
