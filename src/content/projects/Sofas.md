### Project Overview
I collaborated with multiple furniture manufacturers and design brands to deliver high-fidelity 3D product visualizations, catalog renders, and promotional animations for their collections of sofas, chairs, and armchairs.

### Technical Execution
* **Pipeline Automation:** Built a procedural look development and batch rendering pipeline in Houdini Solaris to produce over 7,000 unique variant renders.
* **Mesh Rebuilding:** Reconstructed low-quality CAD files into clean, quad-dominant meshes with customized stitching, wrinkles, and realistic piping.
* **Cloth Simulation:** Simulated cloth draping using Houdini Vellum to achieve natural folds and shapes.
* **Scene Assembly:** Managed automated camera framing and lighting scripts to guarantee consistent compositions.
* **Promotional FX:** Generated a dynamic fabric transition animation showing worn textures morphing into clean, custom upholstery.
