**Project Overview:** The client requested a visually engaging, stylized 3D animation highlighting the modular construction and assembly of conservatory roof panels, mimicking the clean, narrative-driven aesthetic of Airbnb promotional spots.

**Technical Execution:**
* **Custom Tree Generator:** Developed a proprietary generator where leaf animation was simulated using a POP point solver in Houdini, allowing precise, design-driven motion control.
* **Procedural Snow Accumulation:** Designed a Volume VOP-based accumulation workflow to smoothly transition panels and surrounding structures from summer states to snow-covered winter environments.
* **Weather FX Optimization for Solaris:** Created lightweight, packed-geometry rain and snow particle systems in Houdini Solaris to maintain interactive performance during layout and rendering.
* **Full Procedural Assembly:** Rigged the entire conservatory structure, brick laying, and adjacent vegetation to assemble procedurally on screen.
